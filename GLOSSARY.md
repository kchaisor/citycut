# Glossary

- **Exploded axo**: the 3D model view where the site is lifted apart into stacked layers (Ground, Water, Parks, Roads & transport, Buildings, Trees), seen from an isometric-style camera.
- **Layer gap**: the clear vertical space between the top of one exploded layer and the bottom of the next, set by the slider on the Exploded axo card.
- **Named view**: a saved camera position plus the analysis, display and Satellite / 3D model / Drawing settings that were switched on at the time, stored under a name for this site or cut.
- **Viewport export**: a PNG of exactly what is in the display viewport (the 3D or drawing canvas as shown, including analysis overlays), not the surrounding app chrome.
