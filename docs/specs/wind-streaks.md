# Feature: Combined analyses and wind streaks

## What it does

Solar Study, Wind Rose and Viewshed Walk can be on at the same time. With Wind Rose on, the 3D view can show a small diagram of the three most common wind directions for the chosen season: fans of slim, curved, tapered arrows lying flat in the model a few metres above the roofs. This is a diagram of climate averages from the weather station named on the Wind Rose card. It is not a CFD study and it is not live wind.

## What it looks like

Screens this touches: the Analysis bar, the 3D model (Perspective and Isometric), the Wind Rose card and the right-hand column. Since 23 Nov 2026 the right-hand column also carries the Solar Study and Viewshed Walk cards.

### Kelvin's preferences (23 Nov 2026)

1. **Analyses are combinable.** Solar Study, Wind Rose and Viewshed Walk are independent on/off toggles. Clicking one never turns another off. This replaces the pick-one rule (solar-study spec item 8, rewritten the same day).
2. **The bar stays one row; the wind controls live on the Wind Rose card.** (Rewritten 23 Nov 2026, Kelvin's GO at 11:45 AM AEDT on the round-3 analysis-card mocks. It used to say the bar gains a second or third row.) The Analysis bar is the ANALYSIS header, the three analysis toggles and High / Fast, and nothing else. Season, time of day, design winds, the prevailing badge, Flow, Streaks, Pause and the Density slider are on the Wind Rose card in the right-hand column. The solar controls are on the Solar Study card (`docs/specs/solar-study.md`, item 7) and the walk controls on the Viewshed Walk card (`docs/specs/viewshed-card.md`). The left drawer no longer stops short for the bar.
3. **The selected date and season are ink with white text**, not yellow. Yellow stays on the analysis modes, the overlay chips (Shadows, Sun hours, Sun dome, Flow, Streaks) and High / Fast.
4. **Streaks is its own chip, separate from Flow.** On the Wind Rose card the two chips read **Flow** and **Streaks** (they were "3D Wind Flow" and "Wind streaks" in the bar, which wrapped at card width; screen readers still hear the long names). Flow shows or hides the ground-flow. Streaks shows or hides the diagram arrows. Turning Wind Rose on turns both on. Turning one off hides only that one, and the Wind Rose card stays up either way. The ground-flow slider under them is labelled **Density** (it was "Wind streaks", which clashed with the Streaks chip); it is still the 0.5× to 2.5× slider and still shows only while Flow is running in 3D.
5. **One Play / Pause for both.** It sits beside Flow and Streaks on the Wind Rose card and freezes or resumes the ground-flow and the arrows together, so a still view can be exported. With reduced motion set on the computer it starts paused; Play, or "Animate anyway" on the Density slider, starts it.
6. **Flat arrows in perspective, in fans, with no shadows.** Each arrow is a flat, zero-thickness shape: a hairline tail, a slightly thicker neck and a sharp, narrow head, some slightly barbed. Shafts are gentle single C or S swoops, not straight rulers and not sun-dome arcs. They lie on one level a few metres above the typical roofs and foreshorten with the camera; taller towers stand in front of them. No extrusion, tubes, side faces, shading, cast shadows, chevrons or ink outline.
7. **One fan per direction, kept apart.** One fan of 3 to 5 arrows for each of the top three directions, of different lengths and curves, entering from the upwind side and spreading out. The strongest direction gets five arrows and the longest reach; the weakest three and shorter. At most 12 arrows in all. The built arrows are bolder than the mock, and the three fans sit in separate bands so no group cuts across another.
8. **Where the directions come from.** The three most frequent of the 16 compass sectors in the Wind Rose data for the selected season (Annual, Summer, Autumn, Winter or Spring) and time of day (All day, Morning or Afternoon), ranked by how often the wind blows from there after the hours in that window have been kept, with mean speed breaking a tie. A third direction that is much quieter than the first (under a quarter of it, or under one sixteenth of windy hours) is left out. A north wind flows toward the south. With a design wind selected there is one fan, from that design direction at its own speed. There are no month options.
9. **Three soft colours that stay on in B&W.** Strongest `--wind-stream-1` `#6D9BC7` (dusty blue), second `--wind-stream-2` `#8FBEA3` (sage), third `--wind-stream-3` `#C3A6CF` (lilac). They do not go grey in the B&W View Style. Coral, red, orange, pink, navy and azure are avoided because they clash with the building uses and water.
10. **The arrows travel in a loop.** Kelvin, 11:45 AM AEDT, 23 Nov 2026: "animate them moving into the direction, disappearing and then new arrows come again in a loop". This replaces the earlier soft pulse along a fixed shaft.
    - Each arrow drifts downwind along its own curved path. It fades in at the upwind start, growing out of the edge head first, slides toward the far side, and fades to nothing at the sun dome radius (not at the crop / cut boundary). A new arrow then fades in at the upwind start.
    - The arrows in a fan are staggered evenly, so every fan always has a fully visible arrow. A whole fan never pops at once, and the three fans do not pop together.
    - Speed follows each direction's mean speed loosely and stays calm: one loop takes 6 to 11 seconds (28 divided by the mean speed in m/s, kept inside that range), so a faster direction loops sooner.
    - Kelvin, 12:35 PM AEDT, 23 Nov 2026: arrows must not fade or clip at the crop (cut) boundary any more — they may travel outside the cut. The ~7% cut inset and cut-edge fade from earlier in the day are removed. Bound and fade the arrows by the sun dome radius instead, so they sit roughly within the dome. Spread still scales with the dome; arrow length, shaft width and head size stay the same (preference 15).
    - Prefer full travel along that dome-bounded path. A travelling arrow still stops and fades before it would cut across another fan if that is needed to keep the fans apart (preference 7), but do not add a tighter fan-vs-fan clip than that.
    - Pause freezes the arrows where they are, and Play carries on from there. With reduced motion set, they start paused in a still pose with every arrow fully visible, not caught mid-fade. The still pose is the arrangement from preferences 6 and 7.
    - The 2× PNG and the Rhino `.3dm` always use that still pose with every arrow fully visible, whatever the animation is doing at the time.
    - Changing the season or the time of day rebuilds the fans at once, with no animated change. The arrow shapes, colours and fan counts do not change.
11. **Wind Rose card.** (Extended 23 Nov 2026, 11:45 AM AEDT.) Header "Wind Rose" with the season and station years. Then the rose, the station line (name, distance, years), the velocity scale and a "Prevailing streams" list reading "From N · 3.6 m/s" for each direction, with a small flat arrow swatch in the same fill and no ink edge. Under those, in sections: **Season** as one row of five single-line buttons (Annual / Summer / Autumn / Winter / Spring); **Time of day** as one row of three single-line buttons (All day / Morning / Afternoon) with the hour windows shown on the card (Morning 6am–12pm, Afternoon 12pm–6pm, local standard time); **Design winds** as a compact left-aligned group (NE / S / W, or whatever the site has); the **Prevailing** badge on the left under its own label ("S, SSW · 21 km/h"), with the calm share beside it; and **Overlays** as one row of three (Flow / Streaks / Pause) with the Density slider on one row under it. The rose fills the card width, with the card's usual padding around it, and the direction labels stay legible. Time of day, Flow, Streaks and Density stay reachable: the card body scrolls when the window is short of room, instead of shrinking the rose. This holds at 1440×900 and 1440×1080, and when another analysis shares the column. In Plan the Wind speed / Comfort switch and its Arrows toggle sit at the top of the card and work as before; the diagram arrows and the 3D ground-flow still hide in Plan.
12. **Right-hand column.** (Rewritten 23 Nov 2026, 11:45 AM AEDT.) The column is 328 px wide (it was 304), 14 px from the right. Each analysis that is on has a card: Solar Study, Wind Rose, Viewshed Walk, in that order. While any of them is open, Site Intelligence folds to its 48 px header; clicking the header opens it again. With two or three analyses on, the cards work as an accordion: every header stays visible, only the analysis turned on last starts open, and clicking a folded header opens it and folds the one that was open, so one analysis body is open at a time. The column stops 16 px above the Analysis bar (about 748 px of height at 900 tall, 648 at 800), and an open card scrolls inside itself when it is short of room. It is not cut off at 1280 or 1440. The Direct Sun Hours legend is inside the Solar Study card.
13. **Compass letters stay readable.** The sun dome's N / E / S / W and other labels are drawn above the arrows with their halo; the arrows are drawn above the dome's lines.

14. **The fans follow the sun dome radius.** Kelvin, 11:48 AM AEDT, 23 Nov 2026, tightened at 12:35 PM: when the sun dome expands in radius, the wind streaks should expand with it so they sit roughly within that radius, and they fade and are bounded at that radius, not at the crop. While the Sun dome is showing, where the fans sit, how far they travel, and where they fade follows the live dome radius (`docs/specs/solar-study.md`, item 22). A smaller dome pulls the fans in toward the centre; a larger one spreads them back out. When the dome is larger than the sides of the cut (the slider now goes to 2× the cut edge, so well past the crop), the arrows may travel outside the cut rectangle and still fade at the dome. This updates as the Sun dome size slider moves. With the Sun dome off, or Solar Study off, the fans fill the cut as a wind-only view, with no 7% edge inset.
15. **The arrows themselves do not grow.** Kelvin, 11:48 AM AEDT, 23 Nov 2026: "just to make sure the arrows shouldn't grow in size or larger." Every arrow keeps the same length, shaft width and head size at every dome radius. Those sizes come from the cut, as before, never from the dome. Only placement and spread change.
    - Because the arrows keep their size, a dome much smaller than the cut cannot hold a full-length arrow strictly inside the ring. The fans then pull in only as far as they can while staying apart (preference 7 still holds) and while every arrow keeps its full length; they sit roughly within the dome, not strictly inside it. Three directions at right angles pull in least.
    - The 2× PNG and the Rhino `.3dm` use the same spread as the screen.

16. **Morning and afternoon can be split out.** Kelvin, 6:46 PM AEDT, 23 Nov 2026: the Wind Rose card has an All day / Morning / Afternoon control, default All day, so nothing changes until a window is picked. Morning is 6am–12pm (EPW hours 7–12) and Afternoon is 12pm–6pm (hours 13–18), in the station's local standard time, with no daylight saving. Those hours are shown on the card. Hours are filtered before the directions are ranked, and both the rose diagram and the 3D arrows follow the chosen window. The usual arrow rules stay (flat, curved, slim, tapered, no shadows, colour in B&W, drift-and-fade loop, fans follow the sun dome radius, arrows themselves do not grow). The app loads only the site's station file, not the whole national table, so the first open stays quick.

17. **The rose diagram is larger.** Kelvin, 7:29 PM AEDT, 23 Nov 2026, on draft #280: "I think wind rose diagram can be bigger it's a bit small." The diagram fills the Wind Rose card width, with comfortable padding and legible labels. Time of day, Flow, Streaks and Density stay reachable (the card may scroll or reflow). Checked at 1440×900 and 1440×1080 desktop.

18. **Flow can be seen in Plan.** Kelvin, 3:24 PM AEDT, 24 Nov 2026: "with the wind flow animation, i'd like to be able to see this on plan view. however at the moment when i select plan view it changes to the windmap diagram". In Plan with Wind Rose on, the Wind Rose card has a **Plan view** choice, **Wind map | Flow**. Wind map is the default and is exactly the Plan wind diagram as before. Flow turns the wind map off and shows the animated ground-flow and Streaks seen from above, with the same Flow / Streaks / Pause row and Density slider as in 3D; the Wind map-only parts of the card (Wind speed / Comfort switch, Arrows, plan legend) hide. The camera stays in Plan and does not move. The choice is remembered between visits. A saved view stores it, and a Plan Flow view exports what is on screen; the A3 wind sheet swaps the plan heat legend for the Flow speed legend.

## How we'll know it works

Open Southbank (`?lat=-37.8216&lon=144.9645&km=0.5`) in 3D.

- [ ] Click Solar Study, then Wind Rose: both stay on, the shadows and dome stay, and the bar is still one row at 1440×900 and 1280×800 with High / Fast at the right. The Wind Rose card is open and the Solar Study card is folded to its header.
- [ ] Turn both off: the one-row bar stays, with no muted controls and no analysis cards.
- [ ] Solar Study alone and Wind Rose alone each leave the bar at one row and show one open card.
- [ ] Three fans of flat arrows show in blue, sage and lilac, 12 arrows at most, none cutting across another fan, and towers hide the parts behind them.
- [ ] Choose B&W in View Style: the city goes grey and the arrows keep their colours.
- [ ] Flow off hides only the ground-flow; Streaks off hides only the arrows; the Wind Rose card stays. The slider under them is labelled Density.
- [ ] Pause stops both animations; Play starts them again. With reduced motion on, they start paused.
- [ ] The arrows drift downwind along their curves, fade out at the sun dome radius (they may travel outside the cut) and fade back in at the upwind side, a few at a time, taking several seconds per loop.
- [ ] Pause holds the arrows exactly where they are; Play carries on from there. With reduced motion on, every arrow is still and fully visible.
- [ ] Pause mid-fade, then download the 2× PNG: every arrow is fully visible in the image. With Wind streaks on, the Rhino `.3dm` has a "Wind streaks" layer with the same still arrows as flat meshes in "Stream 1" to "Stream 3"; with Wind streaks off it has none.
- [ ] The Wind Rose card lists "From N · 4.8 m/s" style lines that change with the season, then Season, Time of day, Design winds, the Prevailing badge and Flow / Streaks / Pause, each button on one line. Site Intelligence shows only its header, and nothing in the right-hand column is cut off.
- [ ] Time of day starts on All day. The card shows "6am–12pm · 12pm–6pm · local standard time". At Southbank (Melbourne Regional Office), Morning in the year as a whole is mainly from the north, Afternoon mainly from the south and south-south-west; the rose and the 3D arrows both change. Switching back to All day restores the previous streams. The arrows do not grow.
- [ ] At 1440×900 and 1440×1080 the rose fills the card width (about 296 px, with the card's 16 px padding). The N / E / S / W labels are legible. Time of day, Flow, Streaks and Density can be reached, if needed by scrolling the card body.
- [ ] With Solar Study, Wind Rose and Viewshed Walk all on, three headers show and only the last one turned on is open; clicking another header swaps which one is open.
- [ ] With Solar Study and Wind Rose on, drag Sun dome size from 0.5× to 2× cut: the fans move in and out with the dome, fade at the dome (and may sit outside the cut, well past the crop at 2×), and no arrow gets longer, thicker or a bigger head. Turn the Sun dome off: the fans fill the cut as they do with Wind Rose alone, with no 7% inset.
- [ ] Opening Site Intelligence does not move the bar.
- [ ] In Plan with Wind Rose on, Wind map is selected by default and shows the heat diagram. Choosing Flow shows the animated ground-flow and arrows from above, the Overlays row and the Density slider appear, and the camera does not move. Choosing Wind map brings the diagram back.
- [ ] A named-view PNG or A3 sheet shows wind Flow and Streaks in the same colours as the screen (Flow is tone mapped and sRGB encoded in the export, as the screen composer does).

- [ ] Flow streaks over water are drawn at 30% of their strength (set by the water areas, not by depth) and are never hidden by the water surface. A normal PNG and a High quality PNG with Transparent background show the same faint streaks over the harbour as the screen.

## Out of scope

- No CFD and no live wind. No month options.
- No change to the ground-flow model or its streak cap.
- No arrows in Drawing, Plan wind maps or `.ai`. The 2× PNG and the Rhino `.3dm` do include them, as a still frame with every arrow fully visible (preference 10); nothing is animated in the `.3dm`.
- The side-panel HUD work was out of scope for the first draft; it is in scope for the analysis-cards follow-up (preferences 2, 4, 5, 11, 12, 14 and 15; Kelvin's GO, 23 Nov 2026, 11:45 AM AEDT).
- Thinning the sun dome's lines while the arrows are on (allowed by the plan, not needed so far).
